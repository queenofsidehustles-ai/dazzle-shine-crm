# Dazzle & Shine — Lead Specialist Training Plan 🎯

**Role:** Lead Specialist (find businesses → call → close them into cleaning clients)
**Tools:** Dazzle & Shine CRM → **Find Leads** + **Call List**

Welcome to the team! Your job is simple to say and powerful to do: **turn phone
calls into paying cleaning clients.** This guide walks you through exactly how.

---

## 💵 How You Get Paid

Your earnings reward results, so the more you close, the more you make:

| What | You earn |
|------|----------|
| Weekly base (starting period) | **$50 / week** |
| Each lead you close **that pays** | **$20** |
| Each time a recurring client gets cleaned | **$5 residual** 🔁 |

**Important — what "closed" means:** a lead counts as *closed* when the customer
**actually pays** (not just when they say "maybe" or book). Money in the door = you get paid.
The CRM tracks this automatically, so your pay is always fair and clear.

## 🖥️ Getting Started Each Day

1. Log into the CRM with **your own username and password**.
2. In the left menu, under **Get Customers**, click **🎯 Find Leads**.
3. You'll see two areas:
   - **Top:** the search box (to find new businesses)
   - **Bottom:** your **📞 Call List** (the businesses to call)

---

## 📋 Part A — Build Your Call List (5 minutes)

1. In the search box, pick **who to call** from the dropdown:
   - 🏢 Property Manager · 🔑 Realtor · 🛏️ Airbnb Host · 🏘️ Apartment Complex
   - 🧸 Daycare · 🩺 Doctor / Medical Office · 💼 Office Space
2. In **Where**, type the city (e.g. `Orlando, FL`).
3. Click **🎯 Find Businesses.**
4. A list of real businesses appears (name, phone, rating). **Tick the ones you want**
   and click **➕ Add selected to call list.**
5. Repeat with different categories or nearby cities to keep your list full.

> 💡 **Tip:** Start with the **highest-rated** businesses (⭐4.5+). They're
> established, busy, and more likely to need reliable cleaning help.

---

## 📞 Part B — Work the Call List (the main event)

For each business: **tap the phone number** to call, then set the **status** after.

### The Golden Rule
Be **warm, brief, and confident.** You're not begging — you're offering a busy
person a solution that makes their life easier. Smile while you talk; they can hear it. 😊

### Opening script (Property Manager / Office / Daycare)
> "Hi, this is **[Your Name]** with **Dazzle & Shine**, a local Orlando cleaning
> company. Who handles your cleaning or janitorial services?"

→ When they tell you the name, **write it down in the notes field!** (e.g. "Ask for Maria")

### Discovery (get them talking)
- "Do you currently have a cleaning company, or handle it in-house?"
- "How often do your units/offices need cleaning — turnovers, weekly, monthly?"
- "What's working well with your current setup, and what's frustrating?"

### The close (pick one)
- "I'd love to send you a quick quote — what's the best email?"
- "Could we set up a 10-minute walkthrough so I can give you exact pricing?"
- "We have an intro offer for your first clean — want me to lock it in?"

### Handling common objections
| They say | You say |
|----------|---------|
| "We already have a cleaner." | "Totally understand! Can I be your backup for when they cancel or you need extra hands? It costs nothing to have us on file." |
| "Just send info." | "Happy to! What's the best email? And when's a good time to follow up next week?" *(then log a Call Back)* |
| "Too expensive / what's the price?" | "It depends on the space — that's why a quick walkthrough helps. Most clients are surprised we're competitive. When works?" |
| "Not interested." | "No problem, thanks for your time! Mind if I check back in a few months?" *(mark Not Interested, stay kind)* |

## 🏷️ Setting the Status After Each Call

Use the dropdown on each business:

- **Called** — you reached them, conversation happening
- **No Answer** — didn't pick up (try again later)
- **Call Back** — they asked you to follow up (add the date in notes!)
- **Interested** — warm! they want a quote or walkthrough
- **Not Interested** — a no (be gracious)
- **Won 🎉** — they said YES and are becoming a client

**Always add a quick note** (who you spoke to, what they said, next step). Hit 💾 to save.

---

## ✅ What Counts as a "Win"

A **Won** is a business that agrees to become a paying client. Once they actually
pay for their first service, that's your **$20** — and if they're recurring, your
**$5 every clean** after that. 🔁

---

## 🤝 After a "Yes" — The Handoff

- **A home / small residential job** → tell **[Owner]**, who sets up the booking.
- **A commercial account** (apartment complex, daycare, doctor's office, office building)
  → these go to our **commercial cleaning team**. Flag it clearly in the notes and
  alert **[Owner]** so it's routed to the right person.

---

## 📏 Rules & Etiquette (please read!)

1. **Only call businesses** from the Find Leads tool. Never cold-call random
   personal cell numbers — it's against the law and hurts our reputation.
2. **Calling hours:** weekdays roughly **9am–5pm** local time. No early mornings,
   late nights, or weekends.
3. **Always be professional and kind**, even with rude people. You represent Dazzle & Shine.
4. **Never promise pricing you're not sure of** — offer a quote or walkthrough instead.
5. **Log every call.** If it's not in the CRM, it didn't happen (and won't get counted).

---

## 🎯 Daily & Weekly Targets (starting point)

- **Per day:** add ~20 businesses to your call list + make **25–40 calls**
- **Per week:** aim for **3–5 "Interested"** and **1–2 "Won"** to start
- These grow as you get better — and so does your income. 📈
