# Hiring — Copy-Paste Messages

Quick reference for messages you send to applicants. Just copy, paste, and tweak the name.

---

## Indeed → redirect applicant to your website application

**Your website application link:** https://www.dazzleandshinemaids.com/#careers

### Short version (best for Indeed chat)

> Hi [Name], thanks so much for your interest in joining Dazzle & Shine Maids! 🎉
> To move forward, please complete our quick application on our website here:
> 👉 https://www.dazzleandshinemaids.com/#careers
>
> It only takes about 2 minutes. Once we receive it, we'll review and reach out
> with the next step (a short video interview). Looking forward to learning more
> about you!
> — Monica, Dazzle & Shine Maids

### Fuller version (for email or a detailed reply)

> Hi [Name],
>
> Thank you for applying through Indeed — we're excited you're interested in
> joining the Dazzle & Shine Maids team!
>
> Our next step is a quick official application on our website. This is how we
> collect everything we need to consider you, so please complete it here:
>
> 👉 https://www.dazzleandshinemaids.com/#careers
>
> It takes about 2 minutes. After you submit it, here's what happens:
>   1. We review your application
>   2. If it's a fit, you'll get a link to record a short video interview (from
>      your phone or computer — no app needed)
>   3. Then a background check, and welcome to the team!
>
> If you have any questions, just reply here. Thanks again!
>
> Warm regards,
> Monica
> Dazzle & Shine Maids

## Tip: set your website as the Indeed "apply" destination

In your Indeed job post, you can set the application method to your own website
URL (https://www.dazzleandshinemaids.com/#careers) so applicants land on your
internal form automatically — no manual message needed.
