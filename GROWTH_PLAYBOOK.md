# Dazzle & Shine — Growth Playbook

Your step-by-step plan for getting your first clients. Work it top to bottom.

---

## PART 1 — Turn On Speed-to-Lead (do this first)

Your CRM now texts every new website lead instantly AND alerts you to call them
fast. But the texting only works once you connect a texting service called Twilio.

**What to do:**
1. Make a free Twilio account at twilio.com and get a phone number (~$1/month).
2. In Railway, add these 4 variables (I'll walk you through it when you're ready):
   - `TWILIO_ACCOUNT_SID`
   - `TWILIO_AUTH_TOKEN`
   - `TWILIO_PHONE` (your new Twilio number, like +14075551234)
   - `OWNER_PHONE` (YOUR cell, so you get the "call them now" alerts)

> Until Twilio is connected, you'll still get the **email** alert for every lead —
> so you never miss one. The text is just the upgrade.

**The golden rule:** When you get a lead alert, call within 5 minutes. The first
business to call wins the job about half the time.

## PART 2 — The First 10 Clients Checklist

The fastest path to client #1 is NOT ads. It's people who already know you.

### This week (free, warm leads)
- [ ] **Text your old clients** from your 2019–2022 cleaning business.
      "Hi! Great news — I'm back and taking cleaning clients again with my new
      company, Dazzle & Shine Maids. Would love to take care of your home again.
      Want me to send you a quote?"
- [ ] **Tell friends & family** you've launched. Ask them to share your website.
- [ ] **Post in 5 local Facebook groups** (Orlando moms, neighborhood groups,
      buy-nothing groups). Don't spam — be helpful and mention you're booking.
- [ ] **Post on Nextdoor** as a local business. People ask for cleaner recs daily.

### Set up your "storefront" (free, found-by-strangers)
- [ ] **Google Business Profile** — create/claim it. This is the #1 way local
      clients find cleaners. Add photos, service area, hours, and your website.
      (This also becomes the home for reviews later — the foundation of growth.)
- [ ] Make sure your website link is everywhere: Instagram bio, Facebook page,
      email signature, Google profile.

### Lower the risk for first-timers
- [ ] **Founding Client Offer** — a launch discount (like 20% off first clean, or
      $25 off). I can set this up in your Discounts engine in 2 minutes. Use it in
      every post and conversation: "Booking my first 10 clients at 20% off."

### Get reviews from day one
- [ ] After EACH of your first jobs, personally ask for a Google review.
      The first 5 reviews are the hardest and the most valuable. Once you have
      ~10, we turn on the automatic Review Flywheel and it runs itself.

## PART 3 — Thumbtack Plan of Action

Thumbtack is a "pay-per-lead" marketplace. You don't pay for ads — you pay each
time a customer contacts you (or you respond to their request). Leads for house
cleaning typically cost ~$15–$40 each, so the goal is to only pay for GOOD ones
and close a high percentage.

### Setup (week 1)
- [ ] Create a Thumbtack Pro account and complete your profile 100%:
      real photos of finished work, clear bio, your service list, and prices.
- [ ] **Get verified** (background check badge on Thumbtack) — it dramatically
      boosts trust and your ranking. You already understand background checks now.
- [ ] Ask 2–3 of your first happy clients to leave a Thumbtack review. Profiles
      with reviews get chosen far more often.

### Control your costs (this is the key to not wasting money)
- [ ] Set **Targeting Preferences** tightly: only the exact job types you want
      (e.g., standard + deep cleaning), only your real service ZIP codes, and the
      home sizes you actually serve. Tight targeting = fewer wasted lead charges.
- [ ] Set a **weekly budget** you're comfortable with (start small, like $50–$75).
- [ ] Turn on **Promote / Instant Match** only after your profile + reviews are
      strong, so you convert the leads you pay for.

### Win the leads (same rule as Speed-to-Lead)
- [ ] **Respond within minutes.** Thumbtack ranks fast responders and customers
      message several pros at once — first thoughtful reply usually wins.
- [ ] Save **quick-reply templates** so you can respond instantly with a warm,
      personalized message and a clear next step ("I can do Tuesday at 10am —
      want me to hold it?").
- [ ] When a Thumbtack lead is interested, **add them to your CRM** as a lead
      (source: Thumbtack) and send them to your booking form to lock the deposit.

### Watch the numbers
- [ ] Track: leads paid for → conversations → booked jobs. If you're spending
      $100 to get one $150 job, tighten targeting or improve your replies. If
      you're closing 1 in 4, scale the budget up.

> **Reality check:** Thumbtack and Google LSA both work, but they cost money per
> lead and reward fast responses + strong reviews. That's why Part 2 (warm leads
> + Google profile + first reviews) comes first — it makes every paid lead
> cheaper and easier to close.

## The order that wins
1. **Speed-to-Lead on** (don't waste leads you pay for)
2. **Warm network + Google profile + founding offer** (first clients, first reviews)
3. **Turn LSA back on + start Thumbtack** (paid leads, now that you convert well)
4. **Review Flywheel on** (once you have ~10 reviews — growth runs itself)
