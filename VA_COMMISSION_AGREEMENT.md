# Dazzle & Shine Maids — Lead Specialist Commission Agreement

**Between:** Dazzle & Shine Maids ("the Company") and ______________________ ("the Contractor")
**Role:** Lead Specialist — generating and closing new cleaning clients
**Start date:** ______________

## 1. Independent contractor, commission-only
The Contractor is an independent contractor (1099), **not an employee**. Compensation is
**100% commission** — there is no base salary or hourly wage. The Contractor sets their own
hours and methods.

## 2. Residential commissions (self-generated leads only)
- **$20** for every one-time residential clean the Contractor closes.
- **$5** for every completed recurring cleaning — ongoing, for as long as that client stays
  active and the Contractor remains engaged with the Company.
- Applies **only** to leads the Contractor personally generates. Leads from the Company's
  **paid advertising** (Google Local Services Ads, Meta/Facebook ads, or any other paid
  source) do **not** earn commission.

## 3. Commercial commissions (per account closed)

| Account monthly value | Landing bonus (one-time) | Monthly residual |
|-----------------------|--------------------------|------------------|
| Under $1,000 / month  | $50                      | $10 / month      |
| $1,000 – $2,500 / month | $100                   | $20 / month      |
| $2,500 / month and up | $150                     | $30 / month      |

The **landing bonus** is paid once, when the account's first invoice is paid. The **monthly
residual** is paid each month the account remains active and the Contractor remains engaged
with the Company.

## 4. Momentum bonus
**$100** for any calendar month in which the Contractor closes **10 or more paid deals**.

## 5. How commissions are earned and paid
- A lead/account counts as "closed" only when the **customer has actually paid** (money
  received), not merely booked.
- A lead counts as the Contractor's only if it was **attributed to the Contractor at the time
  it entered** the Company's system.
- Commissions are paid **biweekly**, after the customer's payment has cleared.
- All residual payments (recurring and commercial monthly) **stop if the Contractor stops
  working with the Company**.

## 6. Good faith
Both parties agree to track leads and payments transparently in the Company's CRM. This
agreement may be updated by mutual written consent.
