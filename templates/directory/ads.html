Job adverts you can post

You are the employer, so you can post these free on Indeed, Facebook groups, Craigslist or Nextdoor. Akye can’t — third parties have to pay to sponsor, which is Indeed’s own rule. Your own opening is the one that posts for nothing.

Point the advert here

Your apply page{{ apply_url }}

On Indeed choose “apply on company website” and paste that in. Applicants land in your own hiring pipeline — video interview, background check, onboarding — instead of in an Indeed inbox you have to work through by hand. Add ?src=indeed to the end if you want to know which advert produced which person.

Which version to use

Your settings say you work with {{ '1099 independent contractors' if model == 'contractor' else 'W-2 employees' }}, so {{ 'the first' if model == 'contractor' else 'the second' }} advert is yours. Change it in Settings → Business if that’s wrong.

Do not mix the two. An advert that offers a contractor a set schedule, training, supplies and a supervisor is describing an employee. If the classification is ever questioned, the words you advertised are evidence — and the back taxes and penalties land on you, not on Indeed and not on us.

Where to post, for free

  • Indeed — three free postings a month. Sponsoring is optional, not required, because the job is yours
  • Facebook groups — cleaning and housekeeping groups in your area. Read each group’s rules first; some remove recruiting posts
  • Nextdoor — free business posts, and local by design
  • Craigslist — the gigs section is free in most places
