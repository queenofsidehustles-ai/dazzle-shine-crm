Questions cleaning company owners ask

The ones that come up on nearly every call.

What does {{ PRODUCT }} actually do?
It runs the operating side of a cleaning company: quoting and booking customers, scheduling jobs, assigning or broadcasting them to cleaners, working out what each cleaner is owed per job, keeping checklists and photos as proof the work was done, and tracking what you kept on each job after pay and fees. Hiring is built in too — applications, interviews, offers and onboarding.
Do my cleaners need to download an app?
No. They get a text with a link. They tap it and the job is there — the address, how to get in, what they earn, the checklist, and a Navigate button that opens their own maps app. No account, no password, nothing to install. Somebody you hire on Tuesday can work on Wednesday.
How is this different from ZenMaid, Jobber or Housecall Pro?
Those are scheduling tools with pay bolted on. {{ PRODUCT }} is built the other way round: the hiring pipeline, per-job cleaner pay and proof of work are the product, and the calendar is the easy part that everybody already does well. If your problem is finding cleaners and working out what you owe them on a Friday, that is what this is for.
Is there really a free plan?
Yes, and no card is needed. Two cleaners, twenty jobs a month, your own booking page, and the whole job flow from booking through to getting paid. Pro is ${{ plans['pro']['price'] }} a month when you outgrow it.
Do I get a website?
You get a booking page, which is the part of a website that actually earns money — your name, your colours, your prices, and a form that puts the job straight into your CRM. Put the link in your Facebook bio or your Google listing. On Pro you can drop it into the website you already have with one line of code.
Does it handle contractor pay and 1099s?
It works out what each cleaner is owed — by percentage, hourly rate, or a flat amount you set per job — keeps a running total for the year, and collects W-9s during contractor onboarding. It is a record-keeping tool, not a payroll provider — it does not pay anybody or file anything with the IRS, and it takes no view on whether somebody is a contractor or an employee. That decision is yours.
I do landscaping, not cleaning. Will it work?
Probably. Anything where you send a person to an address, pay them for the job rather than the hour, and need proof it was done, fits the same shape. The wording says "cleaning" in places because that is where it was built and tested. Try the free plan and tell us what does not fit.
What happens to my data if I leave?
It is yours. You can export your customers, jobs and pay records, and ask for the account to be deleted. That is written into the terms, not just promised here.
