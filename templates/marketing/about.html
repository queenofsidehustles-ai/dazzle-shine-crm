Why it exists

Running a cleaning company is quoting, chasing, scheduling, covering call-outs and working out what everybody is owed, on top of the cleaning. We wanted one place that did that the way a cleaning company actually works, so that is what {{ PRODUCT }} is. All three of our companies use it every day.

It doesn’t replace the work. You still answer the phone, knock on doors and show up. It just means fewer Sunday nights with a spreadsheet.
