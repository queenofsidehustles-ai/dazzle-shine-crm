This describes what {{ PRODUCT }} does with personal information. It is written to be read, not to be survived.

Two different roles

This is the part most policies muddle, and it decides everything else.

  • About you, our customer — your name, your email, what you pay us. We decide what to do with that, and this policy governs it.
  • About the people in your CRM — your cleaning customers, your cleaners, your applicants. That is yours, not ours. You decide what is collected and why; we hold it and process it on your instructions. If one of them asks what is held about them, you are the one who answers, and we will help you do it.

What is stored, and how sensitive it is

Being specific here matters more than being brief, because some of it is genuinely sensitive:

  • Your cleaning customers — name, address, phone, email, notes, and how to get into their home. Access notes are the most sensitive field in this software.
  • Your cleaners and applicants — contact details, work history, availability, interview answers, signed agreements, pay records, and any documents you upload, which can include W-9s and background check results. Uploaded documents are stored in your database, reachable only through links that expire.
  • Jobs — addresses, prices, times, checklists, and before and after photos taken inside people's homes.
  • Messages — texts and emails sent through the software, kept so there is a record of what was said.
  • Money — what was charged and what was paid. Card numbers are never stored by us; Stripe holds those and we keep only the last four digits and the card type.

What we do with it

Run the service. Send the messages you tell it to send. Take payment for the subscription. Fix faults. Nothing else.

We do not sell it, rent it, or use it for advertising. We do not use your business data to train AI models.

Where it goes

Some of it has to leave our servers to do its job — a text has to reach a phone company. The full list is on the who we share data with page, and we will tell you before adding anyone to it.

AI features

Some optional features send text to an AI provider: translating a message into Spanish, and drafting social posts. Only the text you asked to be processed is sent. Your customer list, addresses, pay records and documents are never sent to an AI provider, and the provider does not train on it.

How long it is kept

  • While your account is open, everything stays until you delete it.
  • If you close your account, you have at least 30 days to export. After that it is deleted from the live system.
  • Backups take longer. We keep nightly encrypted backups for 30 days, so deleted data can persist in those for up to a month before it rotates out. Saying otherwise would be untrue.

How it is protected

  • Encrypted in transit, and databases encrypted at rest by our host.
  • Each business's data is in a separate database schema. It is not filtered apart by a query somebody could forget to write — one company's software physically cannot see another's rows.
  • Passwords are hashed and never stored readable. Nor are password-reset links: only a one-way scramble of them, so a stolen backup contains no working links.
  • Sign-in attempts are rate-limited, and repeated failures are blocked.
  • Saved integration keys are encrypted before storage.
  • Nightly backups, encrypted, kept off our hosting provider — and tested by restoring them.

No system is perfect. If there is a breach affecting your data, we will tell you what happened, what was affected and what we are doing, within 72 hours of finding out.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your data, correct it, or have it deleted, by emailing {{ SUPPORT_EMAIL }}. Depending where you live you may have specific rights — in California under the CCPA, in the UK and EU under the GDPR — and we will honour them whether or not they strictly apply to us.

If you are one of our customers' cleaners or clients and want to know what is held about you, ask the cleaning company directly. It is their record and they control it. If you cannot reach them, contact us and we will try to help.
