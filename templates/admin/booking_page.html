What it is

It is a page on the internet, already built, where your customers book you themselves. They choose what they want cleaned, see your price straight away, pick a date, and it lands in your Bookings list. No phone call, no back-and-forth, no quote to write out.

You do not have to build it and you cannot break it. It already exists, it already uses the prices you set in Pricing and the colours you set in Business. The only thing left to do is put the link where people will see it.

Where to put it

  • Your Facebook or Instagram bio — the "link" field. This is usually the one that brings the most bookings.
  • Your Google Business Profile — as the appointment link.
  • Your email signature — "Book a clean: {{ booking_url }}"
  • Texts to customers who ask for a price. Instead of writing out a quote, send the link and let them price it themselves.
  • Flyers and van signage — it is short enough to type.

How to use it

  1. Copy the line above.
  2. Open the page on your website where you want the booking form — usually a page called "Book" or "Contact".
  3. Paste it in. In Wix, Squarespace, WordPress or GoDaddy this means adding a block called Embed, Custom HTML or Code, then pasting the line into it.
  4. Save and look at the page. The form appears, and grows to fit — you do not have to set a height.

If your website person does it for you, send them that one line and nothing else. It is all they need.
