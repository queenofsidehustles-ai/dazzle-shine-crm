Cleaner Pay

What a cleaner earns for one hour of work — the same rate for everyone, on every job type. A job's pay is its hours × this rate, split between whoever is assigned. One cleaner takes the whole amount; two split it and each finish in half the time, so the hourly rate is identical either way.

Because pay comes from the hours and not the price, discounting a job no longer reduces what the cleaner earns — the discount comes out of your margin instead. Each job's page shows labor as a % of that job's price so you can see when it's getting thin.

Changing this affects jobs that haven't been paid yet. Anything already paid stays as it was.
