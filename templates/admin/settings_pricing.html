{# Four paragraphs of explanation for one number, in front of somebody who has owned the software for ten minutes. It read as something to study rather than something to fill in. One line says what it is; the rest is behind the arrow for whoever actually wants it. #}

What one cleaner is paid for one hour of work.

How a job's pay is worked out

A job's pay is its hours × this rate, shared between whoever is assigned to it. One cleaner takes the whole amount; two split it and each finish in half the time — so the hourly rate is the same either way.

Because pay comes from the hours rather than the price, giving a customer a discount doesn't reduce what your cleaner earns. The discount comes out of your side, not theirs.
