{# Correcting what a booking has actually taken in. Every booking made before payments were stored as an amount records only that it was paid — true against whatever the price was that day. Raise the price afterwards and the old total exists nowhere, so the CRM has to ask rather than guess. This one number drives the balance, the charge button, the payment link and the invoice, which is why it is editable in both the paid and the unpaid branch of the Payment card. #}
💵 Money actually received: ${{ '%.2f' % collected }} — correct it

What has really landed for this job, deposit included and tips excluded. Enter less than the ${{ '%.2f' % (booking.price or 0) }} price and the difference becomes a balance you can charge.
