Test clean before hiring

Plenty of owners want to watch somebody clean before trusting them in a customer's home. Turn this on and the conditional offer says so up front, in English and Spanish, rather than springing it on somebody at the door.

Pay them for it. A test clean on a real customer's home is work, and unpaid work that benefits the business is the kind of thing that turns into a wage claim. Book it as an ordinary job — they are already on your team at this point, so you can assign it or broadcast it like any other.

Both optional, and both go in the offer email when you fill them in — “$40 for about 2 hours”. A candidate who knows the number before they say yes is a candidate who turns up.

Optional. Whatever you already use — Calendly, Google, anything with a link. It goes in the offer email so they can pick a time without a round of texts. Leave it blank and you arrange it yourself.
