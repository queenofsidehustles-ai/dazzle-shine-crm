Why there are two sets, and the placeholders you can use

A missed call, a voicemail, a text nobody answered, a form on your website, a quote request that went quiet — however they reached you, the conversation afterwards is the same one.

There are two sets because these are two different conversations: someone you never spoke to, and someone you already quoted. Apologising for not connecting reads badly to a person you priced up last week.
