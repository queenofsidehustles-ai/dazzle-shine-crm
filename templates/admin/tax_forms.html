What this page is and isn't

These totals are labor paid — the amounts recorded against each completed job. Tips are excluded, because they're the customer's money passed through rather than the business paying for services. Both are tracked separately in payroll.

A 🔒 means Stripe verified that person and holds their tax details for its own reporting. That is not the same as you holding a W-9, and whether Stripe files 1099s for you is a setting in your Stripe Dashboard → Connect → Tax forms, not something this CRM controls. Anyone paid by Venmo, Zelle, cash or check never went through Stripe at all.

Nothing here is filed for you and none of it is tax advice. Take these numbers to your accountant.
