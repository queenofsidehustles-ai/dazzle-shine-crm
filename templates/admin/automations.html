These jobs don't run on their own. Something outside has to wake them up on a timetable, and right now nothing is. Reminders aren't going out and balances aren't being charged.

Set this up — free, no card, about 10 minutes

You are going to sign up to a free website whose whole job is to visit an address on a schedule. That's it — it's a very reliable alarm clock. You do this once and then never again.

  1. Go to cron-job.org and make a free account. Confirm the email it sends you.
  2. Click Create cronjob.
  3. Title: type the name of the job you're setting up — for the first one, Day-before reminders.
  4. URL: copy the address from the matching card below and paste it in. Each card has its own — they are not interchangeable.
  5. Schedule: pick Every day and choose a time, unless the card says hourly — then pick Every hour.
  6. Open Advanced. Set the request method to POST (not GET — it will look like it worked and do nothing).
  7. Still in Advanced, find Headers and add one. Name: X-Api-Key. Value: your REMINDER_API_KEY, copied exactly from your Railway settings.
  8. Save. Then press Run now — you should see a green result, and this page will say Running next time you reload it.
  9. Repeat for each card below. Six jobs, six cronjobs.
Two things go wrong, and both look like nothing happening: the method is left on GET, or the X-Api-Key has a stray space at the end. If a job still says “Never run” after a Run now, check those two first.

How to set these up

Each job is an address on this server that something outside has to call on a schedule — a free service like cron-job.org does it. Every call needs your reminder key sent as an X-Api-Key header, so nobody but you can trigger them.

Full address: {{ base }}/api/<job> · method POST. Run the hourly one every hour; the daily ones once each morning, after people are awake and before the day starts.

Two independent signals on this page. The green and red badges come from runs the CRM has recorded since this page shipped. The 🔎 notes are worked out from your actual bookings — jobs that came and went with no reminder, balances still sitting uncollected — so they can tell you about weeks before any of this existed.
