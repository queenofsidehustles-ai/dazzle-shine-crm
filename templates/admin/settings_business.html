What this means, and how to do it about 20 minutes, once

Right now your customers' emails arrive from our address. That works, and plenty of businesses leave it alone. If you would rather they came from hello@yourbusiness.com, you have to prove to the email system that the domain is yours — otherwise anyone could send email pretending to be you, which is exactly why spam filters check.

  1. Do you own a domain? If your email is @gmail.com, you don't yet — you would need to buy one (about $12 a year at Namecheap, GoDaddy or Cloudflare). If your email is already @yourbusiness.com, you do.
  2. Add it at Resend. Sign in at resend.com → Domains → Add Domain → type your domain.
  3. Copy the records it shows you. Three or four rows, named things like DKIM and SPF. They are just text — you are copying, not writing.
  4. Paste them where you bought the domain, under DNS or DNS Records. One row at a time, copying each value exactly.
  5. Wait, then check. Back at Resend, the domain turns Verified — usually minutes, sometimes a few hours.
  6. Only then change the box above to “Yes”, and set the from-address you want.
Switching to “Yes” before Resend says Verified stops your emails arriving — quotes, receipts and confirmations all just stop, with no error to tell you. If email goes quiet after changing this, put it back to “Not yet” and everything resumes.
