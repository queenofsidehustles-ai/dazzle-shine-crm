What a job of each size ought to pay. Not for contractors.

🔒 Don't send this to a cleaner

This is your starting point for deciding what to offer, not a promise to anyone. Each figure is that home's typical hours × the labor rate in Settings → Pricing.

Real pay is set per job and will often differ from this. A long clean on a discounted recurring plan is worth well under what this table computes — that gap is exactly why pay is set per job. A cleaner holding this chart would read the difference as being shortchanged.

What the cleaner sees is the offer: the home and one dollar amount, before they accept.

Using this: it's a sanity check on what to offer, and on whether a price leaves enough room. Set the actual pay on the booking itself — that figure is what the offer promises, what the claim page shows, and what payroll pays.

Where it goes wrong: the arithmetic here doesn't know about discounts. A recurring plan priced well below one-off rates will compute a pay figure that eats most of the price. Money → Job Economics flags those; this table won't.
