💡 How your pay works

You're paid ${{ '%.0f'|format(labor_rate) }} for every hour of work in a job — the same rate on every job type, for every cleaner. A job's pay is simply its hours × ${{ '%.0f'|format(labor_rate) }}.

Your pay does not depend on what the customer is charged. If we discount a job to win the work, that comes out of our side — your pay doesn't change. It's set by the work, not the price.

Working with someone else? The job's hours are shared between you, so you each earn ${{ '%.0f'|format(labor_rate) }} an hour and finish in about half the time — then you're free to take another job.
