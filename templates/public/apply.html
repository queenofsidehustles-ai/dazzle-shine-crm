Background Check Requirement

All applicants who advance past the phone interview will be required to complete a background check as a condition of working with us.

You are responsible for obtaining and paying for this check directly (typically $20–$30). We will send you the link and instructions after your interview.

We take the safety of our clients and their homes seriously. This step protects everyone — including you.

If you have a background check from Care.com, Checkr, FDLE, or any other provider completed within the last 12 months, paste a shareable link here. We'll review it and may not need to request a new one.
