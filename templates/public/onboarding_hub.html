How you get paid

You're paid a flat rate for each job — one fixed amount for the whole job. Every offer shows you the home and your exact dollar amount before you accept, so you can decide whether it's worth it to you. The amount is set by the size of the home and the type of cleaning.

When two cleaners are assigned the job together, the pay is split between you — you each get your own amount for your share, shown in your own offer — and you finish the home in about half the time, which leaves you free to take another job.

You're not on a clock — the amount doesn't drop if you work quickly.
