✨ Service Standards
▼
  • Arrive 5–10 minutes early to every job — never be late to a client's home.
  • Ring the doorbell or knock before entering, even when you have a key.
  • Wear shoe covers or remove shoes at the front door.
  • Move furniture carefully — never drag items; lift and replace.
  • Use the correct product for each surface (no bleach on stone, no ammonia on hardwood).
  • Leave no streaks or product residue — buff surfaces dry.
  • Do a final walk-through of every room before leaving the property.
  • Take a before-and-after photo of each room and log it in your job notes.
💬 Client Communication
▼
  • Be friendly, professional, and respectful in every client's home at all times.
  • Text the office when you arrive at a job and again when you leave — every single job.
  • If the client is not home, complete the job and immediately text the office when done.
  • Never discuss pricing, discounts, or other clients with anyone.
  • Do not use the client's Wi-Fi, eat their food, or use any personal items.
  • If a client makes you feel uncomfortable or unsafe, leave and call the office immediately.
  • Do not accept cash or gifts from clients without notifying the office.
⚠️ If Something Breaks or Goes Wrong
▼
  • If you accidentally break or damage something — stop immediately. Do not hide it.
  • Take a clear photo of the damage right away before touching or moving anything.
  • Call or text the office right away — do NOT handle it with the client directly.
  • If you notice pre-existing damage when you arrive, photograph it before you start cleaning and notify the office.
  • Never agree to pay a client out-of-pocket — all damage claims go through the office.
  • Document every incident in your job notes the same day it happens.
📅 Scheduling & Punctuality
▼
  • Confirm every job the night before — text the office by 8 PM to confirm you're on for tomorrow.
  • Running late? Text the office immediately — we handle notifying the client.
  • Need to cancel or reschedule? Give at least 24 hours notice so we can rebook.
  • A no-call / no-show (without notice) is grounds for immediate removal from the schedule.
  • Three last-minute cancellations may result in contract termination.
  • Never contact clients directly to reschedule — all scheduling goes through the office.
💰 Pay & Your Responsibilities as a Contractor
▼
  • As an independent contractor, you are responsible for your own taxes — we do not withhold.
  • Track your mileage for tax purposes — use a free app like MileIQ or Stride.
  • Pay is processed on the schedule provided by the office. Ask if you're unsure when payday is.
  • You must mark your jobs as complete on the same day to be paid for them.
  • Never quote prices or negotiate rates directly with clients — all pricing is set by the office.
  • Questions about invoices or pay go to the office, not the client.
🧴 Supplies & Safety
▼
  • As an independent contractor, you bring your own supplies and equipment to every job.
  • Keep all products properly labeled — never mix chemicals (e.g., bleach + ammonia = toxic).
  • If a client requests a specific product, check with the office before using anything new.
  • Always wear gloves when using cleaning chemicals — protect your skin.
  • Dispose of trash bags, soiled rags, and waste properly before leaving each property.
  • Do not use a client's vacuum, mop, or tools without explicit permission.
